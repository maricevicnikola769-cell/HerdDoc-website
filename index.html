Rodoslov i inbriding

Pedigree and inbreeding

Rodoslovno stablo, koeficijent inbridinga, udeo gena svakog pretka i upozorenje kad se predak ponavlja u rodoslovu.

Pedigree tree, inbreeding coefficient, each ancestor's share of genes and a warning when an ancestor repeats in the pedigree.

Pametno parenje

Smart mating

Za svaku ženku najmanje srodni mužjaci i očekivani inbriding potomka. Plan za celo stado sa granicom rizika koju sam biraš.

For every female the least related males and the expected inbreeding of the offspring. A plan for the whole herd with a risk limit you choose.

Expected birth by species, heat, meat and milk withdrawal periods, vaccinations, treatments and your own reminders – nothing slips.

Kako radi

How it works

Računar je glavni, telefon je uz tebe u štali.

The computer is the main record, the phone goes with you to the barn.

Instaliraj na računar

Install on your computer

Upiši grla ručno ili uvezi postojeću tablicu iz Excela. Roditelje upiši jednom – program sam slaže rodoslov.

Enter animals by hand or import an existing Excel table. Enter the parents once – the program builds the pedigree.

Upari telefon QR kodom

Pair the phone with a QR code

Na telefonu instaliraj HerdDoc i skeniraj kod sa računara. Uparuje se samo jednom, a može više telefona.

Install HerdDoc on the phone and scan the code from the computer. You pair only once, and several phones can be paired.

Radi u štali

Work in the barn

Upiši parenje, porođaj ili lečenje na telefonu. Sve se samo usklađuje sa računarom, šifrovano.

Record a mating, birth or treatment on the phone. Everything syncs with the computer by itself, encrypted.

Tvoji podaci ostaju tvoji

Your data stays yours

Rodoslov dobrog stada vredi mnogo. Zato HerdDoc čuva podatke na tvom računaru, a ne na tuđem serveru.

The pedigree of a good herd is valuable. That is why HerdDoc keeps your data on your own computer, not on someone else's server.

  • Na tvom računaru. Stado, istorija i podešavanja su u tvom folderu Dokumenti.
  • On your computer. Herd, history and settings live in your Documents folder.
  • Šifrovano pri usklađivanju. Pre slanja telefon i računar zaključaju podatke ključem (AES-256) koji postoji samo na tvojim uređajima.
  • Encrypted when syncing. Before sending, the phone and computer lock the data with a key (AES-256) that exists only on your devices.
  • Radi i bez interneta. I računar i telefon rade normalno i kad veze nema.
  • Works offline. Both the computer and the phone work normally without a connection.
