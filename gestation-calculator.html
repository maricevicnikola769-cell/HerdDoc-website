Gestation length in farm animals

The calculator uses the average gestation length. The actual birth depends on breed, age, nutrition and litter size, so a few days earlier or later is normal.

How the calving date is calculated

Add the average gestation length to the breeding date – 283 days for a cow. A cow inseminated on 1 March is due around 9 December.

Heat check

If the animal did not conceive, she usually comes back into heat after one cycle: about 21 days for cows, goats, sows and mares and about 17 days for ewes.

For the whole herd

This calculator is for one animal. HerdDoc calculates due dates for the whole herd as soon as you record a mating, lists what is coming up and reminds you on your phone too.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a cow pregnant?

On average 283 days, usually between 275 and 290 days.

How long is a sheep or goat pregnant?

About 150 days for sheep (Romanov around 140) and about 150 days for goats.

How long is a sow pregnant?

On average 114 days – 3 months, 3 weeks and 3 days.

How long is a mare pregnant?

About 340 days on average, normally 320 to 360 days.
